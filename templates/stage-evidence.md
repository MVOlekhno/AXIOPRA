# Stage Evidence Dossier Template

## Method or idea

Name the practice precisely and identify ambiguous variants.

## Historical origin

What problem led to the method, who developed it, and how did it evolve?

## Primary sources

For every source record title, author or organization, date, stable reference, access date, supported claim, and evidence limitations.

## Industry cases

For each real case describe context, scale, adoption form, reported outcome, measurement method, confounding factors, and source quality.

## Benefits

Separate demonstrated outcomes from plausible mechanisms and marketing claims.

## Failures, limitations, and criticism

What went wrong, where did the method fail, and what conditions made it unsuitable?

## Common misuse and anti-patterns

How do teams imitate the terminology while losing the underlying discipline?

## Corrections and mitigations

What did practitioners change to address the failures?

## Alternatives

Which competing approaches may be better under which conditions?

## AXIOPRA adoption decision

State what is adopted unchanged, adapted, rejected, or left experimental, with reasons and confidence level.

## Falsification and review

What evidence would change the decision, when must the dossier be reviewed, and who owns that review?
